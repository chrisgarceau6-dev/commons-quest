# Privacy

**Short version: your food log never leaves your phone.**

- **No account.** Commons Quest doesn't ask for an email, password or student ID.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Your name, character, goals, usual dining hall, diet, allergies and food log are stored only in your browser's local storage. They're never sent to a server; there isn't one.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, ads, cookies or third-party scripts.
- **What the app downloads:** the app itself and the public menu and hours files, from GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard web server logs (like any website host). See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Backups are yours.** "Back up my data" saves a file to your phone. We never see it.
- **Deleting your data:** clear the site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the home-screen app.

**Health and allergen information.** Nutrition and allergen data comes from UMass Dining's published labels. Recipes and ingredients can change. If you have a food allergy, always confirm with dining staff before eating. Commons Quest isn't medical or nutrition advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about goals that are right for you.

**If this ever changes.** If an opt-in feature to share anonymous, aggregated stats is ever added (for example, to help UMass Dining reduce food waste), it will be off by default, explained in plain language, and this page will be updated first. Individual logs will never be sold or shared.

*Commons Quest is an independent student project and isn't affiliated with UMass Amherst or UMass Dining.*
