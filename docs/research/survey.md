# Research sprint: survey + interviews

Post the survey on Yik Yak / r/umass with a Google Form, and hold five 10-minute conversations (2 gym-goers, 2 students with allergies or a diet, 1 freshman). Goal: evidence for or against the core bet before the public launch.

## Survey (5 questions, under 1 minute)
1. How often do you eat at a UMass dining hall? (daily / few times a week / rarely)
2. Do you try to hit any food goal, like protein, calories, or a diet such as halal or vegetarian, or avoid an allergen? (check all that apply)
3. When you walk into a dining hall, how do you decide what to eat? (open text)
4. Have you ever used an app to track what you eat? If you stopped, why? (open text)
5. Would you try a free app that shows what to eat at your hall to hit your goals, with your allergies filtered out? (definitely / maybe / no). Leave your email if you want early access.

## Interview prompts
- Walk me through the last time you ate at a dining hall. How did you pick?
- What's annoying about it? What have you tried?
- (Show the app.) What would make you open this tomorrow? What would make you delete it?

## What we're testing
| Belief | Signal it's true | Signal it's false |
|---|---|---|
| Students with goals, diets or allergies struggle to use the dining data | Many say they guess or check items one by one | Most say "I just eat what looks good" |
| Logging fails because it's slow | "Stopped because it took too long / couldn't find the food" | "Stopped because I didn't care" |
| A game would bring them back | Interest in streaks and bosses during demos | It's seen as childish or distracting |
